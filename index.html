The fire of consciousness, for the sixteenth year

Chit, consciousness. Agni, fire. Chidagni gathers five evenings of discourses, namasankirtanam, harikatha, and Carnatic and devotional music at Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan in Mylapore, from Tuesday 6 to Saturday 10 October 2026.

The Sri Vishnu Mohan Foundation and Sri Gnana Advaitha Peetam are not-for-profit organisations working to bring people together, guided by the self-transforming philosophy explained by Her Holiness Sri Sathguru Swami Gnanananda Sarasvathi Ma. This edition is organised jointly with Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Chennai Kendra.
