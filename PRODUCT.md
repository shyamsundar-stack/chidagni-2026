# Chidagni 2026

**Register:** brand. This is a landing page for a festival. The design is the product.

## What it is

A one-page invitation and programme for the 16th edition of Chidagni, The Fire of
Consciousness Festival: five evenings of lectures, namasankirtanam, harikatha and
Carnatic music, 6 to 10 October 2026, at the Mini Hall, 2nd Floor, Bharatiya Vidya
Bhavan, Mylapore, Chennai. All are welcome. Every session is streamed on Zoom and YouTube.

Jointly organised by the Sri Vishnu Mohan Foundation, Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan and Sri
Gnana Advaitha Peetam.

## Who it is for

1. **Chennai rasikas and families.** Many over 50. Often on mid or low-end Android over
   patchy mobile data. They want to know who is on, when, and which hall (the Mini Hall
   this year, not the Main Hall).
2. **The diaspora.** Watching the stream from other timezones. They need the countdown
   in IST to be right wherever they are, and the Zoom room one tap away.
3. **People who forward things.** This audience shares on WhatsApp constantly. The
   printed pages are the unit of sharing, not the URL.

## What success looks like

- Someone lands, learns the dates and venue, and finds their evening inside ten seconds.
- Someone forwards the invitation to a friend without leaving the page.
- Nobody has to ask "which hall" or "how do I watch from abroad".

## Jobs the page does

| Job | Where |
|---|---|
| Establish what and when | hero |
| Say who is behind it | about |
| Answer "who is on, and when" | schedule |
| Get remote viewers to the stream | watch |
| Get local viewers to the right hall | visit |
| Let anyone forward an invitation | share sheet on the poster strip |

## Constraints

- **Brand is fixed.** Palette, wordmark, photograph, ornaments and artwork are all
  extracted from the client's invitation PDF. Not open to reinterpretation.
- **No build step.** Plain HTML, CSS and JS, deployed to GitHub Pages.
- **Content is fixed** by the printed invitation. Where it is silent or unclear, the
  README says so under "Before the client shares it".
- **Single theme.** No light/dark toggle. The ember-and-paper alternation is the
  invitation's own (dark cover, white schedule pages), joined by its black-red-black bar.
